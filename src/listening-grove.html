WANDER AT YOUR OWN PACE

A few ways in.

Walk with W A S D. Arrow keys move forward and back, and turn. Drag the scene to look. Hold Shift to walk faster. On a phone, hold the round joystick.

Click an instrument, or press E when you are close. Clearings has a button for every place. The ◉ button switches walking and the view from above.

N opens field notes. Escape closes the current panel. A standard gamepad can move and look; A explores, B closes, and Start opens Clearings.

Sound begins only when you choose Sound on. The harp uses quiet synthesized tones. Reduced-motion preferences are respected; the visual instruments still respond to their buttons.

This is a fictional grove. Its balance and lights are simplified visual experiments. Notes are browser-local, with no account or cloud sync. Export them to keep a separate copy.
