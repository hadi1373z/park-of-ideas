Take your time.

Drag the landscape to orbit. Scroll to move closer. Choose a place in The journey, or click its paper structure. Fold controls work with a mouse, touch, or keyboard arrows on a focused slider.

To walk, choose ↟. Use W A S D, arrows to turn, or the touch joystick. Drag to look. Press E near an unlocked fold. ◈ returns to the whole park. On a standard gamepad, sticks move and look, A explores nearby and Start opens The journey. In panels, D-pad moves between controls, the left stick adjusts a focused slider, A activates buttons, and B closes.

Folded places and your reflection are saved only in this browser. There is no account, cloud sync, or sound. Reduced motion removes ambient flights and camera transitions. These are playful paper mechanisms rather than a physical origami simulation.
