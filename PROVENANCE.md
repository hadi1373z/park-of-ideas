# Source provenance

Extracted on 2026-10-22 from City of Ideas, commit 44dced96fe98e9d29607edca818b9e273149d622.
Source: https://github.com/hadi1373z/city-of-ideas

The current shared-city park builder, public data, origami models, lessons, and park tools are the source of this edition. This project replaces the embedded city messaging adapter with its own Three.js renderer and input controller. It omits houses, the chapter mountain, the private House bridge, city tool frames and city-specific travel controls. Origami spawn coordinates are converted from city space to park-local space; paper geometry is retained. The catalogue, source citations, missions, reading routes and Atlas reader are retained.

Public source files were copied selectively. No localStorage contents, credentials, private notebooks or runtime backups were copied. The original City of Ideas repository is unchanged by this extraction.

Skyfold Park was added on 2026-10-25 as a fourth independent experience. Its paper landscape, folding puzzles, interface and journey model are newly authored for this project. It reuses only the bundled Three.js runtime, standard gamepad mapping and existing folded-paper creature meshes. No external artwork, generated image assets, private reflections or browser progress are included in its source.
